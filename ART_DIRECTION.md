# Burger Run Art Direction

## Visual North Star

Burger Run should look like a premium mobile runner inside a neon cyberpunk fast-food factory. The reference image establishes a dense, glossy, high-contrast world with a cinematic rear camera, glowing UI, industrial food machines, dramatic lighting, and readable lanes.

The art direction should avoid plain colored boxes, generic test props, and flat web-demo presentation. Even placeholder geometry should be dressed with bevel-like layering, emissive strips, metallic materials, animated lights, and food-factory silhouettes.

## Palette

Primary:

- Burger gold: `#ffbf3f`
- Flame orange: `#ff6a1a`
- Ketchup red: `#ff2f2f`
- Electric blue: `#24d6ff`
- Gunmetal: `#10141c`
- Charcoal black: `#07090d`

Secondary:

- Mustard yellow: `#ffd84d`
- Lettuce green: `#67ff72`
- Neon magenta: `#ff3df2`
- Steam white: `#dff8ff`

## Materials

- Glossy dark floor panels with bright blue and orange lane lighting.
- Metallic obstacles with black, yellow, and red warning panels.
- Emissive signs and factory trims.
- Gold coins with burger embossing.
- Sauce hazards with shiny red/yellow/orange material.
- Characters use burger ingredients plus tech wear: buns, patty layers, cheese, lettuce, gloves, jackets, crowns, robotic trims.

## Environment Motifs

- Conveyor belt runner track.
- Grill fire bays.
- Fry stations with orange sparks.
- Overhead sauce injectors.
- Giant ketchup and mustard pipes.
- Robotic arms.
- Neon signage.
- Steam vents.
- Warning crates.
- Background portal frames and factory gantries.

## Character Silhouettes

Classic Burger:

- Warm bun top, sesame seeds, cheese, patty, lettuce.
- Tech jacket or belt styling through dark lower body geometry.
- Orange/gold glow trail.

Bacon Beast:

- Bulkier bun stack, bacon strips, red warning lights.
- Stronger shoulder/arm silhouette.
- Flame and grill styling.

Robot Burger:

- Metallic bun plating, blue emissive circuits, visor-like detail.
- Cooler electric effects.

King Burger:

- Crown geometry, gold trim, cape-like back panel.
- Brighter prestige glow.

## HUD Style

- Glassmorphism panels with translucent black fill.
- Metallic angular borders.
- Cyan edge highlights.
- Gold/orange number emphasis.
- Animated powerup dock.
- Large readable mobile typography.
- Button states should glow, press, and transition smoothly.

## Camera Composition

- Rear-follow third-person perspective.
- Player occupies roughly 20 to 25 percent of screen height.
- Track horizon shows deep factory detail.
- Lanes remain readable even when environment detail is dense.
- Camera gently lags and increases field of view with speed.

## Effects

Use lightweight effects built from simple reusable primitives:

- Speed streaks.
- Coin shimmer.
- Impact sparks.
- Steam puffs.
- Sauce splash particles.
- Shield glow.
- Magnet ring.
- Ketchup Rush red trail.

Effects must be bounded and pooled where possible during gameplay.
